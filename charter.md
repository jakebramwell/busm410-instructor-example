# Gap Charter

Filled in at CP1.

## Problem statement

Hospital stays by Texas residents are followed by a readmission within 30 days 9.6% of the time, compared with 8.6% in the best-performing states. We're 95% confident the true gap is between 0.8 and 1.2 percentage points. That's roughly 700 to 1,100 avoidable readmissions over three years, costing about $9 to $15 million in this dataset. The gap is too large to be random.
